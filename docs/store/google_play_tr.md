## İngilizce mağaza girişi (en-US)

Play Console → Mağaza varlığı → Ana mağaza girişi → **Çevirileri yönet → Kendi çevirilerinizi ekleyin** → English (United States). Görseller Türkçe girişten alınır.

**App name (30):** `Learn English at Home`

**Short description (80):**

Learn English vocabulary for Turkish speakers: 1,280 words, offline and ad-free.

**Full description (4000):**

Learn English at Home helps Turkish speakers build English vocabulary step by step, from A1 to B2. Spend a few minutes a day; the app plans the rest. Completely free, no ads, works offline.

LEARN LEVEL BY LEVEL
• 1,280 essential English words from A1 to B2, grouped into topic units
• A short placement test starts you at the right level
• Unit and level exams unlock new content as you progress

REMEMBER WHAT YOU LEARN
• Spaced repetition brings words back right before you forget them
• Difficult words are collected so you can practise them anytime
• Turkish meaning, pronunciation, audio and example sentences for every word

FUN PRACTICE
• Flashcards, multiple choice, listening, spelling and fill-in-the-blank
• Matching and letter-ordering games
• Instant feedback on every answer

STAY MOTIVATED
• Daily goal, streaks and streak freezes
• XP and badges
• Daily reminder at the time you choose

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account needed
• Fully offline, no internet permission
• No ads, no tracking; your data stays on your device
